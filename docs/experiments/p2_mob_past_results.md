## Overview

All prior GS-JSO component evidence comes from two synthetic trained-data families — MatrixCity Small City Aerial (Phase 1, pseudo/rule-label semantics, PSNR ~20-22) and the 3D BAG Amsterdam Jordaan CityGML scene "synthetic-B" (Phase 2, strong synthetic GT, PSNR ~40) — plus a long FlatCity (FC-S4..S6E) series that is almost entirely L_mutual redesign/audit. Across this body, L_mutual is the strongest and most consistent mechanism (it verticalizes wall normals on both real-scale datasets and is a genuine geometric rotation, not just relabeling), but it has a recurring downside: it regresses Stage-3 CityGML validity (val3dity) on simple buildings in Phase 2 and drifts terrain/GroundSurface on FlatCity, which is why FC-S5/S6 spent their whole budget redesigning it (best outcome: a "terrain-off" variant A8 that only barely beats no-mutual). L_structure cleanly tightens group-normal variance and is the top val3dity condition in Phase 2, yet its primitive-level effect vanishes under strong supervision; L_sem is non-destructive but never independently moved a downstream metric and was never cleanly isolated outside Phase 1. Critically, **no component has any result on real aerial imagery, on clean curated labels, or against a density-corrected facet baseline** — every "improvement/regression" below is synthetic and most are blind/oracle splits on tiny building counts.

## Per-component verdicts

**L_sem (semantic CE).** Prior evidence says L_sem is reliably non-destructive — on Phase 1 it adds a semantic head with PSNR/geometry held at parity and reaches mIoU 0.635 (2.5x chance) under rule-GT, gradient-isolated to the semantic logits. Its known failure modes are (a) it has never been shown to clear its own go-target (0.75) or to move any downstream Stage-3 metric, (b) on Phase 2 it is saturated (mIoU ~0.97, accuracy stuck ~21%) because RGB≈semantic by construction, so the metric under-measures it, and (c) the one attempt to add semantic-side coupling (L_semcal, FC-S6E) actively hurt reconstruction (F −0.035, roof_cov −0.18). It was never independently ablated anywhere in the FlatCity series.

**L_mutual (mechanism 1, intra-primitive).** This is the strongest, most consistent and best-evidenced component: it verticalizes wall normals on both real-scale synthetic datasets (P1 19%→91%, P2 28%→79%) as a genuine geometric rotation (P1 horizontal-normal prims 2.2x; P2 corner tilt 16.5°→2.7°), tightens intra-group normals (P2 −14%) and gives the best Stage-3 face IoU (0.214→0.240). Its dominant failure mode is downstream and terrain-related: it **regresses CityGML val3dity** on Phase 2 (−3.8pp, concentrated on simple gable/flat boxes via wall over-merging) and **drifts terrain/GroundSurface** on FlatCity (full mutual cuts ground_cov −0.074 and nearly doubles B104 height error) — the entire FC-S5/S6 effort showed the original mutual *hurts* mean F (−0.014) and only a hand-tuned "terrain-off" variant (A8) barely recovers (+0.008, near-even win/loss), with neither directional (sem2geo) nor joint (semcal) refinements beating it.

**L_structure (mechanism 2, inter-primitive plane alignment).** Prior evidence is split by dataset: on Phase 1 it cleanly hits its target (σ_normal_intra −45%, σ_coplanar −16%) and is orthogonal to L_mutual (doesn't touch Wall-vert), while on Phase 2 its primitive-level normal effect **vanishes** (+1%) yet it is the **top Stage-3 val3dity condition** (+2.3pp, best geometric F1, fewest non-planar errors), helping complex/hip/tri-slope roofs most (+10-17pp). Its failure modes are that its primitive-level mechanism is unreliable under strong photometric supervision (redundant when normals are already aligned), it regresses simple/flat buildings (−16pp, over-tuning), and — critically — it has **zero independent measurement in the entire FlatCity series**, where it was kept disabled (w=0.0) pending a clean L_mutual decision.

**Joint (Both = L_mutual + L_structure).** Prior evidence shows the two mechanisms co-exist without interfering (Phase 1: Both preserves Mutual's Wall-vert 88.3% and restores Structure's σ-reduction that Mutual-alone had wrecked, −36%), and on complex Phase 2 roof types Both can win (tri-slope +15.3pp). But there is **no measured synergy**: Phase 2 Both (54.2%) is ≤ Structure-alone (55.0%), so Structure is mostly *cancelling* Mutual's regression rather than adding to it, and the FlatCity joint attempt (semcal) was screened out entirely (D3_KEEP_A8_LEGACY). The "circular/co-reinforcing" hypothesis remains unproven.

## 한계/공백 (limitations & gaps)

- **실데이터(real aerial) 전무.** 모든 trained 증거는 합성 데이터(MatrixCity synthetic, 3D BAG synthetic-B, FlatCity synthetic)뿐이다. 실제 항공영상(TUM/Seongsu)에서 L_sem/L_mutual/L_structure 어느 것도 측정된 적이 없다 — P2 make-or-break가 필요한 1차 이유.
- **클린 라벨(clean curated-GT) arm 부재.** Phase 1 semantics는 GT depth/normal 기반 rule-based pseudo-label이고, Phase 1의 핵심 부작용(Mutual이 σ_normal_intra를 +46% 악화시키는 "L_sem↔L_mutual 충돌")은 *rule-GT artifact로 추정*되어 "curated Phase-2 GT에서는 사라질 것"이라 주장만 됐을 뿐, 깨끗한 라벨로 검증된 적이 없다. 실데이터 의미 라벨 생성 코드 자체가 레포에 **없다**(GSJSO_loss_audit), 즉 textureless-roof 복원 메커니즘(L_sem-normal + L_normal_align)은 실데이터에서 미검증.
- **density-corrected / facet 단위 비교 부재.** Stage-3 비교는 합성 GT 대비 val3dity·face IoU 위주이며, 면(facet) 밀도를 보정한 baseline(예: sequential MVS+RANSAC, density-corrected facet)과의 정량 비교가 한 번도 측정되지 않았다("no sequential MVS+RANSAC baseline measured").
- **L_sem·L_structure의 독립 on/off가 큰 데이터에서 미흡.** FlatCity 전 시리즈(S4-S6E)에서 L_sem은 한 번도 분리 측정되지 않았고 L_structure는 전 구간 w=0으로 차단됨. 즉 mechanism-2의 *FlatCity 메트릭이 존재하지 않으며*, mechanism-2의 효과는 Phase 2에서도 Stage 2 group 정보가 Stage 3로 전달되지 않아(인터페이스 단절 C2) 부분만 측정됨.
- **절대 수치 신뢰 불가 — 상대 순위만.** Phase 2 val3dity는 v3(pre-fix, baseline 40.5%)와 v4(post-fix, 52.7%) 두 버전이 공존하고, bbox_margin 1줄 버그가 +10-17pp를 왜곡했으며 GT_convex 천장(96.2%)을 인용한 파일은 **커밋되지 않았다**(디스크에는 pre-fix 76.3%만 존재). 따라서 절대 천장·절대 pass-rate는 미결정이고 상대 랭킹만 신뢰된다.
- **소규모·blind/oracle split 한계.** FlatCity 결론은 10개 건물 oracle(building-id 입력 허용) per-building이고 win/loss가 near-even(A8 3W/3T/4L)이며, roof-complex(B3/B123/B126, F~0.43-0.59)와 B6 height error는 미해결. Phase 2도 blind split이지만 합성+RGB=semantic 텍스처가 L_mutual을 under-measure한다.
- **시너지·downstream 인과 미규명.** Both에서 L_mutual+L_structure 시너지가 관측되지 않았고(Both ≤ Structure), L_mutual이 *왜* simple building의 val3dity를 떨어뜨리는지(over-merge 메커니즘) 인과가 정량적으로 닫히지 않았다. directional(sem2geo)·joint(semcal) 개선 시도는 모두 A8 legacy를 넘지 못했다.
